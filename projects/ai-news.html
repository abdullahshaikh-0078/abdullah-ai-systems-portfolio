Engineering Highlights & Architectural Decisions

🐘

Native pgvector 0.8.6 on PostgreSQL 18

Compiled official pgvector C extension against PostgreSQL 18 MSVC headers. Enables zero-overhead in-database vector similarity calculations using the cosine distance operator (<=>) directly combined with relational joins and ACID guarantees.

PostgreSQL 18 pgvector C-Extension Zero Latency
✨

Google Gemini AI Embeddings & Synthesis

Vendor-agnostic provider abstraction generating 1536-dimensional semantic representations via Google Gemini. Grounded story synthesis preserves every original content item while generating factual summaries and actionable takeaways.

1536 Dimensions Gemini Flash Provider Abstraction

Transparent 6-Signal Deterministic Ranking

Replaces black-box recommendation algorithms with an objective, fully auditable 6-signal scoring model: Recency (25%), Relevance (25%), Authority (20%), Coverage (15%), Engagement (10%), and Format Diversity (5%).

Diversity-Enforced Editorial Curation

Configurable diversity caps prevent news feeds from being monopolized by a single corporate publisher or topic. Enforces maximum items per topic and per source to guarantee broad, balanced technical coverage.
